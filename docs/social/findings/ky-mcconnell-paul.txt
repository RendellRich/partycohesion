Mitch McConnell and Rand Paul both represent Kentucky as Republicans. In the 119th Congress so far (2025 to now, through Nov 12, 2026), they cast opposite votes on 126 of the 751 roll calls both voted on (16.8%). A typical pair of same-party senators from one state splits on about 3% of votes. Since 2011, they have split on 13% to 29% of votes each Congress. A split is a fact about the record, not a judgment: a roll call can't show a senator's reasons.
