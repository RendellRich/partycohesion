Every state has two senators who answer to the same voters. When they're from the same party, they rarely split: the typical same-party pair voted opposite ways on 2.7% of shared votes in the 119th Congress so far (2025 to now, through Nov 12, 2026). When they're from different parties, the typical pair split on 73%. That gap has been there in every Congress since 2007.
