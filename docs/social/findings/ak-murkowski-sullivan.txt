Lisa Murkowski and Daniel Sullivan both represent Alaska as Republicans. In the 119th Congress so far (2025 to now, through Nov 12, 2026), they cast opposite votes on 122 of the 859 roll calls both voted on (14.2%). A typical pair of same-party senators from one state splits on about 3% of votes. Since 2015, they have split on 6% to 44% of votes each Congress. A split is a fact about the record, not a judgment: a roll call can't show a senator's reasons.
